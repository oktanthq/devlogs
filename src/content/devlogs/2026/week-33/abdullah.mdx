This is the first entry in the Oktant engineering log. It is the earliest public record of a platform being built in the open, and it deserves to start at the start: what Oktant is, and the ground rules the team is building under.

### What Oktant is

Oktant is a platform for defining, running, and observing analysis pipelines. The tagline the team keeps returning to is *analyze, deploy, observe* — one line that describes the entire arc of the product. Enabling analysis is upstream of everything else.

### Why the log exists

The engineering log is not a marketing channel and it is not a changelog. It is the team's public notebook: what was believed in a given week, what was decided, what ran, and what broke. Three engineers, one entry each, every week, committed as source of truth. The project should be as legible from outside as it is to the people building it.

### The ground rules

Three rules structure every decision:

1. **The model comes first.** Interfaces are chosen deliberately; nothing is improvised at the seams.
2. **The platform assumes the worst of steps.** Work is untrusted until a plan says otherwise.
3. **Correct paths are easy paths.** Whatever friction exists in the tools must exist for a reason.

Foundation week one is about structure, not volume. The decisions that seem slow now are the ones that stop being slow in month six.
