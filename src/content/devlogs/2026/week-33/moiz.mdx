The instinct of most young platforms is to postpone infrastructure until a problem physically demands it. This week the team talked through the opposite habit that Oktant is intentionally keeping: plumbing first, demand second.

### The bet

Infrastructure — process management, isolation, structured logging, resource accounting — is not feature work and does not forgive retrofits. The bet is that building the boring substrate first turns later milestones from emergencies into integrations. The risk is that time gets spent on parts of the platform nobody touches for months.

### The line

The line between readiness and waste is drawn by dependency: build the infrastructure the next milestone will step on, not the infrastructure the platform will eventually deserve. For the foundation milestone that means the process and isolation primitives under the runtime — not the multi-region control plane our imaginations keep suggesting.

### What this week produced

No feature. Two deliberate decisions instead: the substrate gets time budgeted every single week, and infrastructure debt gets logged the week it is spotted rather than the week it is paid. Both decisions are cheap to make now and expensive to reverse later.

Infrastructure is the least romantic part of building a platform. It is also the only part that is unforgiving.
