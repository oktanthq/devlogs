A platform that analyzes but cannot act is a library. A platform that acts but never analyzes is a script. M2 is where Oktant learns to look before it leaps.

### The analysis boundary

Analysis reads the IR and writes conclusions. This week formalized that boundary: analyzers are pure functions from the work graph to a list of findings. They cannot execute, spawn, or reach for the network. That purity is what makes them cheap to run and safe to trust.

### First analyzers

Two primitive checks landed:

- **Cycles** — a work graph with a cycle is a pipeline that will hang. The cycle detector reports the exact loop, positioned back to source.
- **Missing inputs** — a step that declares an input nothing produces. Pathological in a good compiler, impossible here to forget now.

### The posture

Findings are typed: error, warning, note. The engine treats them strictly because the analytics, not the tools, are the point. Nothing executes until the cheap checks pass — this is analysis acting as the gatekeeper, which is the role we want it to grow into.

The primitives are deliberately small. Their job is to prove the seam works; the interesting analyzers come after the runtime exists to be analyzed.
