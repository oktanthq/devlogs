## What is Oktant?

Oktant is an engineering platform for the complete software lifecycle. It brings building, deploying, and understanding software into one cohesive environment instead of scattering the workflow across disconnected tools.

## The name

Oktant is named after the **octant**, an old navigational instrument used to measure angles between the horizon and objects in the sky. It represents orientation, measurement, and finding a clear direction — ideas that shape the platform too.

## The product

Oktant is organized around three pillars:

- **Build** — code, repositories, PR reviews, code analysis.
- **Deploy** — infrastructure, environments, releases, and production.
- **Observe** — logs, metrics, traces, alerts, and system health.
